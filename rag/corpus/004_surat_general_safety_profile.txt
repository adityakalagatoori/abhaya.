Surat scores 80/80 on this site's safety index, placing it, per the source, among India's safer major cities alongside Navi Mumbai. The page frames most concerns as practical rather than violent-crime-related.

Low-risk areas noted: Gopi Talav and other family-oriented public areas draw local families and are described as safer public spaces; heritage sites such as Surat Castle are recommended for daylight visits.

Medium-risk concerns noted: pickpocketing in crowded markets and transport hubs, especially during festivals; traffic chaos and unpredictable driving, with app-based cabs recommended over informal transport; overcharging/scam risk with transport and shopping.

Higher-risk issues noted: unsafe tap water (bottled water recommended); monsoon flooding between June and September affecting mobility; and isolated riverfront and beach-adjacent areas after dark, described as posing "isolation risk" rather than a specific crime statistic.

Practical guidance quoted/paraphrased from the source:
- Use app-based transportation, especially after dark.
- Stay in well-reviewed neighborhoods near commercial areas.
- Avoid wandering alone at night on quiet roads.
- Avoid isolated riverfront/beach stretches after sunset.
- For women travelers specifically: prefer daytime arrival, verified transport, and avoiding isolation after dark.

Relevance note: This is a general tourist-safety index/advisory, not an incident report. It supports general guidance about isolated/unlit areas after dark but contains no specific incident data and should not be cited as evidence of a specific crime event.
