The article, based on National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) 2022 data, challenges Gujarat government claims about declining crime rates.

Custodial deaths: Gujarat recorded the highest number of custodial deaths nationally for the third consecutive year, with 24 deaths in 2022 (compared to 12 in 2019-20 and 17 in 2021-22).

Crimes against children: Gujarat ranked second nationally in murders of children under six years old, with 45 killings recorded in 2022.

Violence against women: On average, 45 women are raped every month, more than six are attacked with acid, and 260 are murdered in the state per NCRB-derived figures cited in the article.

Surat-specific finding: Surat, described as the economic hub of Gujarat, recorded the highest number of crimes against women among cities in the state. The surge is attributed by the report to rising consumption of IMFL (India-made foreign liquor) and country liquor. A social activist filed an RTI application in September 2023 to obtain crime statistics from 13 of Surat's 36 police stations covering 2015 through 2023, and the resulting data indicated increasing rates of rape and harassment across those stations, with commentary blaming insufficient enforcement of prohibition laws in Gujarat (a legally "dry" state).

Other findings in the same report: low conviction rates for Dalit crime cases (3.065%), rising cybercrime complaints (1.59 lakh complaints registered on Gujarat's 1930 cybercrime helpline between January 2020 and May 2023), and a four-fold increase in incidents promoting communal enmity.
