Indian Railways Security Circular No. 02/2021 (dated 2 March 2021) addresses crime against women in trains and on railway premises, citing approximately 4.6 million women passengers daily nationwide.

Infrastructure & access-control measures specified:
- Install proper lighting in vulnerable areas including platforms, parking areas, and maintenance depots.
- Demolish abandoned structures that lack surveillance.
- Close unauthorized entry/exit points and maintain controlled access to yards and coaching depots.
- Remove encroachments blocking passenger areas.

Technology & monitoring measures:
- Deploy CCTV surveillance in vulnerable zones, including cameras specifically covering women's coaches.
- Use the National Database on Sexual Offenders for area surveillance.

Train-specific and staffing measures:
- Brief escort parties on prevention protocols, especially for night operations.
- Heightened monitoring near toilets, cited as a frequent incident location.
- Verify identity credentials of outsourced personnel.
- Gender-sensitization training for staff; publicize emergency helpline numbers and One Stop Centers.

Relevance note: this is an official (secondarily reproduced) railway security circular. It is generic national guidance, not a Surat-specific incident report, but is relevant background for any route/waiting-point context near Surat railway station (e.g., verified safe-haven pickup near a station, or WalkGuard's last-100m near a station exit). It should not be used to claim any specific Surat station segment currently lacks lighting or CCTV — only that lighting/CCTV/access-control are officially recognized risk factors for railway-adjacent areas.
