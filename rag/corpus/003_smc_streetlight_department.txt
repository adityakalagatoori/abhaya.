The Surat Municipal Corporation's Streetlight Department is responsible for installation, operation, and maintenance of all streetlights on roads within SMC limits, as well as street/road lights on high masts at major traffic junctions.

Streetlight services are provided through a partially decentralized management structure via 8 zone offices, with coordination and policy-making handled through the Streetlight (HQ) department, part of the Light & Energy Efficiency Cell.

Stated responsibilities (quoted from the official page):
- "Installation, operation & maintenance of all streetlight on the roads within SMC limits"
- Management of street and road lights at high masts positioned at major traffic junctions
- "Installation, operation & maintenance of Decorative Luminaries and Poles wherever necessary"
- Energy monitoring and efficiency implementation for streetlight services, including a Centralized Control and Monitoring System (CCMS) for reducing non-operational streetlights and improving uptime

Citizens can file streetlight outage/complaint reports through the online portal (Complaint section under Online Services) or by phone at +91-261-2423750 to 2423756 or +91-261-2422285 to 2422287.

Relevance note: This is an official municipal government source documenting that street lighting maintenance is zone-based and complaint-driven in Surat, meaning outage patterns are locally variable and not uniformly documented in real time. This corpus entry should be used only as general infrastructure-governance context, not as evidence about any specific street's current lighting condition.
