This is the official annual NCRB "Crime in India" publication for reporting year 2022, published by the Ministry of Home Affairs. It is the primary government source of state- and city-level registered crime statistics in India, including categories such as crimes against women (IPC/BNS-defined offences: assault, harassment, kidnapping, sexual offences), crimes against children, and cybercrime.

Use in this corpus: this document is referenced as the authoritative government source underlying secondary reporting (see corpus entry 001, The Federal's analysis of Gujarat's 2022 NCRB figures, including Surat-specific commentary about crimes against women in the city). The full PDF was not reproduced verbatim in this corpus; the entry here records the source's existence, publisher, and scope so that the RAG system can cite the origin of NCRB-derived claims transparently, rather than treating secondary reporting as a primary statistic without attribution.

Note for developers: for a production system, download and parse the actual state/district tables from this PDF (or from data.gov.in's NCRB datasets) rather than relying only on secondary news analysis, to get precise, per-district figures for Surat.
