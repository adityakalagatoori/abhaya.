Ahmedabad traffic police faced public backlash over safety campaign posters urging women to avoid late-night activities to prevent sexual assault. The posters, displayed in the Sola and Chandlodia areas of Ahmedabad, carried statements including "do not attend late night parties, you could be raped or gangraped" before being removed following public criticism.

Police response: Deputy Commissioner Neeta Desai stated the traffic department had only approved traffic-related materials, saying: "The NGO had approached us...such controversial posters were not shown to us and were plastered without our consent."

The posters were reportedly created by an NGO called Satarkta Group without police authorization; the traffic department stated it had sponsored only traffic-awareness content, not women's-safety messaging.

Political criticism: Gujarat AAP contested the state government's women's safety record, stating that "in the last three years, more than 6,500 incidents of rape and more than five rapes per day" had been reported in the state.
